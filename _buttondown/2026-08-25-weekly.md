### [Structured output: stop parsing prose, demand a schema](https://claudecodesessions.com/structured-output-stop-parsing-prose/)

You ask the model to extract fields and it hands you a friendly paragraph wrapped around a JSON blob. You write a regex to fish it out; it breaks on the next response. The fix isn't a nicer prompt — it's constraining the output to a schema and validating it at the boundary.
